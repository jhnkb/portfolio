Hif3a Isoform Analysis During Erythroid Differentiation

This project investigates the expression of different Hif3a transcript isoforms during red blood cell development in mice. Using publicly available SMART-seq2 single-cell RNA sequencing data, I analyzed isoform-specific regions to identify which Hif3a transcripts are expressed across different stages of erythroid differentiation, from BFU-E to CFU-E.

Workflow & Methods

  1. Data Acquisition and Preparation

    Obtained publicly available SMART-seq2 single-cell RNA-seq data from GEO (GSE117228). Organized sequencing data across erythroid differentiation stages, including BFU-E, intermediate populations, and CFU-E.

  2. Read Alignment

    Used STAR to align sequencing reads to the mouse reference genome (GRCm38/mm10) with Ensembl version 87 annotations. Examined alignment statistics and generated BAM files for downstream transcript analysis.

  3. Hif3a Isoform Annotation

    Examined Ensembl GTF annotations to identify four Hif3a transcript isoforms and their genomic coordinates. Identified unique 5' and 3' untranslated regions (UTRs) that could help distinguish isoform expression.

  4. Isoform-Specific Read Quantification

    Used SAMtools to filter alignments based on mapping quality and alignment flags. Converted aligned reads into genomic intervals using BEDTools bamtobed, then used BEDTools intersect to count reads overlapping isoform-specific UTR regions.

  5. Expression Analysis Across Cell Stages

    Used R and dplyr to organize isoform-specific read counts by individual cell and erythroid differentiation stage. Compared expression patterns across BFU-E, intermediate stages, and CFU-E to identify dominant Hif3a isoforms.

  6. Alternative Splicing Analysis

    Used SCASL and STAR SJ.out.tab splice junction files to investigate alternative splicing patterns. Compared observed splice junctions with annotated Hif3a introns to assess support for different transcript isoforms.

  7. Visualization and Interpretation

    Used R to summarize and compare isoform expression across differentiation stages. Examined read coverage and splice junction evidence to evaluate which Hif3a transcripts were most consistently supported by the data.

Results

The analysis showed that Hif3a-002 was the dominant isoform across the examined erythroid differentiation stages. Hif3a-001 was also detected at lower levels, while Hif3a-004 showed little to no support in the isoform-specific UTR analysis.

So far, I have explored Hif3a isoform expression across different stages of erythroid differentiation using single-cell RNA-seq data. I have also examined isoform-specific regions and alternative splicing patterns. The analysis is still ongoing, and no definitive conclusions have been reached.
