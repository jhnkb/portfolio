about me

Hi! I'm John, a Computer Science student at UC San Diego specializing in Bioinformatics. I'm interested in using programming and computational methods to better understand biological problems. Outside of school and research, I enjoy playing tennis, spending time with my dog June, and learning new things.

background

I'm currently an undergraduate researcher at the Li Lab at UC San Diego's Moores Cancer Center, where I work with single-cell sequencing data to study gene expression and cellular development. Through my research, I've gained experience working with R, Python, and various bioinformatics tools. I'm still learning a lot, but that's also what I enjoy about research. There's always something new to figure out.

After completing my undergraduate degree, I hope to pursue graduate studies in bioinformatics to strengthen my computational and statistical skills. My goal is to eventually work in bioinformatics or computational biology.

interests

Outside of academics, I spend a lot of my free time playing tennis. It's one of my favorite ways to stay active and take a break from staring at my computer. I also enjoy spending time with my dog June, who has his own page on this website!

I also enjoy exploring different topics, whether it's learning more about biology, improving my programming skills, or just going down a random rabbit hole about something interesting.
